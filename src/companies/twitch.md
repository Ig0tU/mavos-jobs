## Company blurb

Twitch is a live-streaming platform with large gaming and creator communities. Teams hire for creator outreach, community originals, moderation operations, and community product.

## Remote status

Twitch posts a mix of U.S. remote, hybrid, and office roles. Contractor creator-outreach roles have been listed as Remote (United States) via Greenhouse. Confirm work authorization and timezone on each posting.

## How to apply

Start at [twitch.tv/jobs](https://www.twitch.tv/jobs) or Greenhouse (`job-boards.greenhouse.io/twitch`). Search for Creator, Community, Trust & Safety, and Moderation.
