## Company blurb

Circle (circle.so) is a branded community platform used by creators, coaches, educators, and institutions to run membership communities with discussions, events, courses, chat, and payments under their own brand. Customers include independent creators and large organizations.

The product is built around community operations: moderation tools, member spaces, programming, and creator-economy billing.

## Remote status

Circle is a fully remote company. Public careers materials describe a distributed team across 30+ countries and do not require office presence for most roles.

## How to apply

Open roles are listed on the official careers page: [circle.so/careers](https://circle.so/careers). Apply through the posting (Greenhouse). Confirm timezone and region constraints on each listing before applying.
