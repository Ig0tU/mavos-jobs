## Company blurb

Patreon is a creator membership platform. Creators run paid memberships, community chats, live video, and direct sales for fans. Community and creator-success roles sit next to product, trust & safety, and growth.

## Remote status

Patreon is remote-friendly with a hybrid policy for office-based employees in New York and San Francisco. Many roles are posted as remote for selected U.S. locations. Always read the location line on the individual job.

## How to apply

Apply at [patreon.com/careers](https://www.patreon.com/careers). Community, creator growth, and trust roles appear under marketing, operations, and product-adjacent teams.
