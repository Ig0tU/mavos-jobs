## How to use this list

- Prefer the company career URL over aggregators.
- Confirm **remote vs hybrid**, **country / work-auth**, and **contractor vs FTE** on the posting itself.
- “Talent pool” posts (Keywords Studios) are real hiring pipelines, not guaranteed open seats.

## Standing search queries (official boards)

Use these on the company ATS, not on LinkedIn scrapers:

- `"Community Manager" OR "Community Moderator" OR "Trust & Safety" OR "Creator Community" OR "Player Support" OR "DevRel Community"`
- Boards worth polling weekly: Greenhouse (Twitch, Discord, many games publishers), Ashby (Common Room and startups), Lever, and first-party pages for Circle, Patreon, Buffer, Wikimedia.

## What we will not list

- Roles that only exist on unattributed Telegram / WhatsApp “job dump” channels
- Listings that require the applicant to pay
- Aggregator pages with no path back to the employer ATS
